Native Tank byte compatibility recipe derived from RedBlink's Dune Docker experimental Tank implementation, under the accompanying MIT license. DST generates its compatibility packages locally from the server owner's installed Funcom game content. No Funcom executable or cooked asset is distributed with DST. Retoc and UAssetGUI are downloaded from their pinned upstream releases when needed; Oodle is the pinned runtime dependency used by retoc and is not redistributed by DST.
